Online Services

Learn to complete forms, organise digital documents, and find official public services.

Learn • Practise • Grow

Why this skill matters

Knowing how to use online services can save time and help you access information, education, and public support more independently.

Step-by-step learning guide

Take your time. Practise one step at a time, and ask a trusted trainer for help whenever you need it.

  1. Start from the official organisation website; check the web address and contact details.
  2. Read each form field carefully and prepare only the documents the service requires.
  3. Practise entering information into a sample form before submitting a real application.
  4. Review spelling, dates, phone numbers, and uploaded files before pressing Submit.
  5. Save the acknowledgement or reference number, and use the official portal to check status.

Your practice activity

Use a sample form to practise entering fictional contact details and selecting a document name. Do not enter real sensitive data on an unfamiliar site.

How this skill can empower you

These skills can help with training registrations, education applications, appointments, and other legitimate online services.

Stay safe
Do not share identity or bank documents on an unverified website. Avoid unofficial agents and ask for help if a form is unclear.
