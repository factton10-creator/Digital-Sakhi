Smartphone Basics

Build confidence using your phone independently for everyday life, learning, and opportunities.

Learn • Practise • Grow

Why this skill matters

A phone can help you communicate, learn new things, keep documents organised, and access useful services. You can learn one small task at a time.

Step-by-step learning guide

Take your time. Practise one step at a time, and ask a trusted trainer for help whenever you need it.

  1. Find Settings and learn to adjust text size, brightness, Wi-Fi, and mobile data.
  2. Practise saving a contact, making a call, and sending a message or photo.
  3. Use the official app store when installing apps; check the app name and publisher first.
  4. Use Files or Photos to find a document or picture, and learn how to share it with a trusted person.
  5. Set a screen lock and learn how to lock your phone if it is lost.

Your practice activity

Save one trusted contact, change one setting that helps you, and locate a photo or document on your own.

How this skill can empower you

These skills help you contact support, join learning groups, keep important documents handy, and access services more independently.

Stay safe
Use a screen lock, keep your phone updated, and do not install apps or share access because a stranger asks you to.
