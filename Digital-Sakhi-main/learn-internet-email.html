Internet & Email

Find reliable information, communicate clearly, and use email for study, services, and work.

Learn • Practise • Grow

Why this skill matters

Search and email skills make it easier to learn independently, contact organisations, and apply for opportunities.

Step-by-step learning guide

Take your time. Practise one step at a time, and ask a trusted trainer for help whenever you need it.

  1. Connect to a trusted Wi-Fi network or use mobile data; avoid sensitive transactions on unknown public networks.
  2. Search with a clear question and compare information across reliable sources.
  3. Learn the email inbox, sent, spam, and trash folders.
  4. Practise writing a short subject line, greeting, message, and polite closing.
  5. Before sending, check the recipient address and attachments; do not open unexpected attachments.

Your practice activity

Draft an email asking a training centre about course dates. Add a clear subject and check the recipient address before sending.

How this skill can empower you

Email can support school and college work, job applications, communication with service providers, and access to learning resources.

Stay safe
Never email passwords or OTPs. Verify requests for personal documents and avoid sharing sensitive information with unverified contacts.
